# Supply-Chain-Analytics-Project
# 🚀 End-to-End Supply Chain Operations & Logistics Analytics

An end-to-end data analytics project built to analyze FMCG retail supply chain data, identify high-risk suppliers, track logistics performance, and optimize inventory operations.

---

## 📊 Project Overview
In this project, I performed data analysis on a retail supply chain dataset containing 100+ SKUs across Skincare, Haircare, and Cosmetics categories. The goal was to uncover key insights regarding revenue generation, supplier defect risks, shipping costs, and inventory stock levels to help management make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools Used
* **MS Excel:** Data exploration, formatting, and initial data cleaning.
* **Python (Pandas, Matplotlib):** Data preprocessing, automated cleaning, handling anomalies, and Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA).
* **SQL (SQLite / Pandasql):** Executing analytical queries for carrier performance and supplier risk assessment.
* **Power BI:** Designing an executive dark-themed interactive dashboard featuring KPIs, custom glowing cards, dual-axis demand charts, and dynamic Slicers.

---

## 📈 Key Business Insights & KPIs
* **Total Revenue Generated:** $577.60K across 46,099 units sold.
* **Supplier Risk Analysis:** Identified that certain suppliers (e.g., Supplier 5) had high defect rates (up to 2.66%), flagging them for quality audits.
* **Logistics Optimization:** Evaluated shipping carriers to determine the most cost-effective and fastest shipping routes (Carrier B leading with 43 shipments at an average cost of $5.50).
* **Inventory Management:** Tracked inventory thresholds (Stock levels vs. Order quantities) to prevent stockouts and minimize excess inventory costs.
